Note that on the Pacific coast and further west the start falls around midnight or on the evening of May 8, so many viewers there watch a repeat or the recording of the news. The channels usually repeat key moments in their news programmes during the day.

Which channels to use

  • Perviy Kanal (Channel One) and Rossiya 1, the main national channels, normally carry the parade live.
  • Rossiya 24 broadcasts news around the clock, so you can follow events and replays there.
  • Rossiya RTR — USA and Rossiya RTR — Europe are versions of the Rossiya channel made for audiences abroad; their schedule can differ from the domestic channel.
  • The exact lineup is set by the broadcasters. Open the "Today's schedule" block on the channel page: it shows the programme in your device's local time.

Set it up the evening before

  1. Test the stream. Open the channel on the device you will use and watch for a few minutes.
  2. Plan the screen. A phone or laptop is fine; for a TV see Russian TV on Smart TV, Roku and more.
  3. Mind the early hour. In North America it is the middle of the night, so set an alarm and use Wi-Fi; HD needs about 5 Mbit/s.
  4. Have a backup channel. If a stream lags, open another one: the player also switches to a backup source on its own for the main channels.
