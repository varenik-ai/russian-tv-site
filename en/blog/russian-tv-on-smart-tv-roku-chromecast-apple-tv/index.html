How to Watch Russian TV on Smart TV, Roku, Chromecast, Fire TV and Apple TV (2026)

Short answer: on most TVs, open the TV's web browser and go to russian-tv.com/tv, a page built for a remote control. If your TV or streaming stick has no browser (Apple TV, Roku), send the stream from your phone or computer with AirPlay, Cast or screen mirroring. No app, no registration and no VPN are needed.

The TV page: russian-tv.com/tv

The TV page is a simplified version of the site made for a remote control: big channel tiles you can move across with the arrow buttons, and a quality menu in the player. Type the address once in your TV browser and bookmark it. If typing with a remote is awkward, bookmark the page once and it opens in one click next time. Android users can also send a channel from the Telegram mini-app: press "Connect phone" on the TV page to get a code (valid for 10 minutes), then press "To TV" in the mini-app and enter it; the TV remembers the pairing afterwards.

Samsung and LG

  • Samsung (Tizen). Press Home, open the Internet app, type russian-tv.com/tv, choose a channel. If your remote has a voice button, you can dictate the address.
  • LG (webOS). Press Home, open Web Browser and type the address. A Magic Remote works as a pointer. On older webOS versions the browser may not play live streams; use Cast or AirPlay from a phone instead.

Android TV, Google TV and Fire TV

  • Android TV / Google TV. Open the browser on the TV and go to russian-tv.com/tv. If the TV has no browser, cast from your phone: open the site in Chrome on the phone and use the Cast option.
  • Fire TV and Fire Stick. Open the Silk browser, enter the address, and use the Fire TV phone app as a keyboard if typing is slow.

Apple TV, iPhone and iPad (AirPlay)

Apple TV has no web browser, so use AirPlay: open russian-tv.com in Safari on an iPhone, iPad or Mac, start a channel, press the AirPlay button in the player and choose your Apple TV (or an AirPlay-compatible TV). On an iPhone the player may ask you to press once more before the list of TVs appears. Casting from inside the Telegram app often does not send the picture, so open the site in Safari for AirPlay. As an alternative, iOS also offers system screen mirroring from Control Center.

Chromecast and Google Cast

On a computer, open the site in Chrome, open the browser menu and choose Cast (under "Save, share and cast"), then pick your Chromecast or Google TV. On an Android phone use the Cast option in the Chrome menu. The tab appears on the TV. The phone and the TV must be on the same Wi-Fi network.

Roku

Roku does not offer a general-purpose web browser, so the practical route is screen mirroring. Roku supports mirroring on many current players and TVs (AirPlay for Apple devices and Miracast for some Windows and Android devices), but support depends on the model and the phone, so check your device first. If mirroring does not work on your model, a Fire Stick or a Chromecast plugged into the TV is an inexpensive alternative.

If the picture is not smooth

  • Check the network. About 5 Mbit/s is enough for HD. A cable or the 5 GHz Wi-Fi band is steadier than a weak 2.4 GHz signal.
  • Old TVs. Some older or budget TV browsers cannot play live streams. Then cast from a phone or use a Fire Stick or Chromecast.
  • Casting and VPN. When you cast or use AirPlay, the TV loads the stream itself, so a VPN on your phone does not matter. The site works without a VPN anyway.
  • Pick another channel or quality. The TV page has a quality menu, and the player switches to a backup source when one fails. More in Channel not loading: 8 fixes.

What to watch

Start with Perviy Kanal, Rossiya 1, NTV and the news channel Rossiya 24; for classic cinema try Dom Kino. If you live in North America or the UK, check the time difference first: Moscow time in US time zones, Canada and the UK.

Menus differ between TV models and software versions, so the names above may vary slightly. russian-tv.com only links to streams that are publicly available on the internet; rules for foreign broadcasts differ between countries, and this guide is not legal advice.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I watch Russian TV on a Samsung or LG Smart TV? ▾

Open the TV browser ("Internet" on Samsung, "Web Browser" on LG), go to russian-tv.com/tv and choose a channel. No app, registration or VPN is required.

How do I watch Russian TV on Apple TV? ▾

Apple TV has no browser. Open russian-tv.com in Safari on an iPhone, iPad or Mac, start a channel and use the AirPlay button to send it to the Apple TV.

How do I watch Russian TV on Roku? ▾

Roku has no general-purpose browser, so use screen mirroring from a phone or computer if your Roku model supports it. A Fire Stick or Chromecast is an alternative.

How do I cast Russian TV to a Chromecast? ▾

Open the site in Chrome, choose Cast from the browser menu and select your Chromecast. The phone or computer and the Chromecast must be on the same Wi-Fi network.

What is the TV page and the phone code? ▾

russian-tv.com/tv is a version of the site built for a remote control. It can show a code, valid for 10 minutes, that Android users enter in the Telegram mini-app (press "To TV"); after that the TV remembers the connection.

Do I need a VPN on my TV? ▾

No. The site works without a VPN. When you cast, the TV loads the stream itself, so a VPN on your phone makes no difference.
