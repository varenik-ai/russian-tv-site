Time: Moscow and Germany

Moscow stays on UTC+3 all year. Germany has one time zone but changes clocks: Central European Time (UTC+1) in winter and Central European Summer Time (UTC+2) from the last Sunday of March. The next change is on 28 March 2027. The gap with Moscow is 2 hours from late October to late March and 1 hour in summer.
