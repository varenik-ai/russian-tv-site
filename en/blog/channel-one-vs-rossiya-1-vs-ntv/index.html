Perviy Kanal (Channel One)

Perviy Kanal is the idea of "the first channel" in Russian television and the usual home of the main national New Year programmes. It is known for evening talk and entertainment shows, music projects and the information programme Vremya, which has been on air for decades. If you want a bit of everything, from news to a big weekend show, this is the usual starting point. It is also the channel most families have in mind when they talk about "Russian TV".

Rossiya 1

Rossiya 1 is part of VGTRK and is built around the Vesti brand, which airs several times a day with regional inserts. Besides news it carries political talk shows such as 60 Minut and a large block of Russian-made series, which is why many people watch it for the drama in the evening. If you want a longer news format and regional coverage, or you follow a series, Rossiya 1 is the natural choice. For viewers abroad there are also versions made for audiences outside Russia: Rossiya RTR — Europe and Rossiya RTR — USA.

NTV

NTV, owned by Gazprom-Media, is historically associated with crime and investigative programmes and with action and detective series, alongside its news bulletins. If you like detective stories, the channel's own series and documentary-style investigations, NTV is the closest match. The sister channel NTV Hit carries more series.

How the news differs

All three channels have news bulletins, but they are tied to the state or to state-linked companies, so many viewers abroad watch them for the Russian-language picture of events in Russia and also compare them with other sources. If you only want news around the clock, Rossiya 24 broadcasts non-stop, and TV Centre offers a Moscow-oriented news and talk format.

Which one should you choose?

  • You want news plus entertainment in one place: start with Perviy Kanal.
  • You follow series or want longer news and regional coverage: choose Rossiya 1.
  • You like detective stories and investigations: choose NTV.
  • You do not want to choose: open several channels in tabs; they all stream at the same time and cost nothing. The player switches to a backup source on its own if one fails.
