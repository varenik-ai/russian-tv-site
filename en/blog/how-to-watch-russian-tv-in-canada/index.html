Moscow time in every Canadian time zone

Russian channels run on Moscow time (UTC+3), which does not change during the year. Canada is different: it spans six time zones, and since 2026 the picture has become uneven. According to Moving2Canada, British Columbia, Alberta and Manitoba did not turn their clocks back on November 1, 2026 and now stay on daylight time all year, while Ontario, Quebec, Atlantic Canada and Newfoundland did turn back. Internet speed and mobile data

About 5 Mbit/s is enough for comfortable HD. A stream at that quality can use roughly one to two gigabytes of data per hour, so on a phone with a capped mobile plan it is better to watch over Wi-Fi. If several people stream at home at once, a cable or a stronger Wi-Fi point helps. Turn a VPN off: the site works without one, and a VPN usually adds delay.

Tips for families and older relatives

  • Put it on the home screen. On an iPhone or iPad open the site in Safari, tap Share and choose "Add to Home Screen"; on Android use "Add to Home screen" in Chrome. The site then opens like an app, which is easiest for older relatives.
