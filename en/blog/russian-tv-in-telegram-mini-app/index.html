Russian TV in Telegram: How to Watch Live Channels in the Mini App

Short answer: open t.me/RussiaTVHub_bot/russiatv in Telegram and pick a channel. It is a free Telegram mini app with 57 Russian TV channels: nothing to install, no registration and no VPN. It works on iPhone, Android and Telegram Desktop.

What the mini app is

A Telegram mini app is a small web app that opens inside the messenger. Ours shows the same live channels as russian-tv.com in a layout made for a phone: a list of channels, topic chips at the top, and a player that opens over the list. The colours follow your Telegram theme, so it looks right in light and dark mode.

How to open it

  1. From the bot. Open @RussiaTVHub_bot, press Start and tap the button "Open Russia TV Live". The bot answers in eight languages (Russian, English, Bulgarian, Ukrainian, German, French, Spanish and Turkish).
  2. From the channel. Subscribe to @RussiaTV_Hub_Live: the pinned post has a button for the app and for the website.
  3. By link. Share the address t.me/RussiaTVHub_bot/russiatv with friends and family: it opens the app directly.

Using it

  • Find a channel. Use the search field, or the topic chips: news and general, entertainment, movies, kids, music, hobby and style, sport.
  • Watch. Tap a channel; the player opens. Use the quality menu if the picture stutters: a lower setting helps on a weak connection.
  • If a channel fails. Streams come from third-party sources. Wait a moment or pick another channel; for the main channels the player switches to a backup source by itself. More in Channel not loading: 8 fixes.

Time difference

Russian channels follow Moscow time (UTC+3), which does not change during the year. Work out the gap for your country with Moscow time in US time zones, Canada or the UK.

Saying thanks (optional)

The service is free. If you like it, the "Say thanks" button in the app lets you send a small voluntary gift with Telegram Stars. It is optional and changes nothing in what you can watch.

The channel list and features may change. russian-tv.com only links to streams that are publicly available on the internet; rules for foreign broadcasts differ between countries, and this article is not legal advice.
